Service terms

These terms explain how resume and portfolio services are requested and delivered. The written scope, price, and timeline agreed by email for an order apply to that order.

What the services include

  • Resume refresh: editing and organizing the information you provide into an editable resume copy and PDF.
  • Portfolio website: preparing a responsive personal website from your supplied, verified details and project links, then publishing the approved version to an agreed hosting account.
  • Resume + portfolio: both services above, with consistent details across the deliverables.

Before work starts, we will confirm the requested deliverables and any extra work in writing. A custom domain, paid hosting, third-party subscriptions, or other external charges are not included unless explicitly stated.

Your review and approval

You are responsible for checking names, dates, links, claims, and other facts. No skills, employment, qualifications, or results will be invented. A portfolio is published only after you approve its content. The number of revisions included will be stated in the written scope before work starts.

Payment and delivery

Payment is processed by the payment provider shown at checkout. The page will show the final checkout amount before you confirm payment. Payment unlocks the request form; it does not mean the finished work is automatically published. Work begins after your materials and the agreed scope are received.

Customer materials and account access

You confirm you have permission to share the text, images, and other materials you provide. Never send passwords, API keys, or payment credentials by email. If publishing needs account access, we will agree on a safe method; you retain ownership and control of your accounts and files.

Results and third-party services

The work helps present your experience. It cannot guarantee interviews, employment, traffic, search placement, or results from third-party platforms. Third-party services have their own terms and availability.
