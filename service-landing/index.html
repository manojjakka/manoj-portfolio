Questions before you start

Good to know.

How much does a setup cost?

Tell me which deliverable you need. I’ll send a written quote with the scope and timeline before any work begins. Prices are not listed yet.

Do you guarantee a job or interview?

No. The service helps present your experience. Hiring decisions depend on many factors outside the work provided.

Can you include projects or skills I haven’t done?

No. The content should reflect work and skills you can verify and discuss confidently.

Will the website be live automatically?

Not automatically. I can provide publishing guidance or agree on setup help in your quote. Hosting and domain costs, if any, will be explained separately.
