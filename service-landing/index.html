Questions before you start

Good to know.

How much does a service cost?

The current introductory prices are US$5 for a resume refresh, US$10 for a portfolio website, and US$15 for the complete setup. Each is 80% below its listed standard price. INR checkout converts the selected USD price using the latest published reference rate.

Do you guarantee a job or interview?

No. The service helps present your experience. Hiring decisions depend on many factors outside the work provided.

Can you include projects or skills I haven’t done?

No. The content should reflect work and skills you can verify and discuss confidently.

Will my portfolio get its own live link?

Yes. For a portfolio or complete setup order, after your payment is confirmed and you approve the finished draft, I publish it and send you its personal live URL. Any custom domain or ongoing hosting costs are agreed with you beforehand and may be separate.

Does payment publish my website immediately?

No. Payment confirms your order and unlocks the intake form. I’ll contact you to confirm your materials and scope, prepare a draft, and publish a portfolio only after you approve it.

Where can I read the policies?

See the service terms, refund policy, and privacy policy linked at the bottom of this page.
